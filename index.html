01 / HANGAR

About

I'm a Ph.D. student at UC San Diego, working with Prof. Yufei Ding. I did my undergraduate degree in Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University.

My research sits between systems and machine learning: compilers, GPU code generation, and the tooling that lets LLM agents do real engineering work. The most recent project is TritonGym, a benchmark for agentic workflows that write Triton GPU kernels.

I like building things end to end, from a kernel's network stack to a grading platform to the small voxel world on this page.

My roommate and labmate is Zhuo Chen (Tsinghua Yao Class), who is also the best cook in the building.
